About

From Sfax to Louisville.

An engineer trained in mathematics, now building deep learning for the signals that matter.

IntroFoundationsEducationJourney

I'm a fully funded Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of Louisville, where I'm also a Graduate Research and Teaching Assistant.

My path started with mathematics: two intensive years of preparatory engineering studies, a top-1% admission to Tunisia Polytechnic School, and a multidisciplinary engineering degree spanning signal processing, optimization, computer architecture and software.

Along the way I worked on plasma experiments, satellite imagery and electric-vehicle integration — and kept finding that the hardest problems arrive as signals in time. Today I build deep learning for multivariate time-series in health and finance, and I care that every model is accurate, efficient and honestly evaluated.

I speak English and French fluently, and Arabic natively.

Data Engineer Intern at the Digital Research Centre of Sfax (CNRS), in collaboration with the German Aerospace Center (DLR). Simulated satellite data and built deep learning models to improve 3D point-cloud reconstruction from 2D imagery.

2023 · CNSTN, Tunisia

Where physics met AI

Selected for the MENA Plasma and Fusion Physics School (MPFuS 2023), a one-week intensive school hosted by Tunisia's National Center for Nuclear Sciences and Technologies (CNSTN) with nuclear research partners from France, China and Switzerland. We ran hands-on plasma experiments, then trained models on our own measurements to predict material phases for experiments we had not run — the first time I brought physics, chemistry, mathematics and AI together.
