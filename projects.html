Finance · Optimization · Deep learning

Classic vs AI Portfolios on the S&P 500

A leakage-free, walk-forward benchmark that runs classic portfolio theory, evolutionary optimization, end-to-end deep learning and a time-series foundation model through one backtester — with transaction costs and statistical testing.

Approach

  • Point-in-time S&P 500 membership, monthly rebalancing, transaction costs
  • Classic baselines: equal weight, Markowitz mean-variance, minimum variance, risk parity
  • Genetic algorithm, plus a Wisdom-of-Crowds ensemble
  • End-to-end portfolio network (attention-LSTM + Transformer) trained on a Sharpe–CVaR loss
  • Amazon Chronos-2 forecasts, zero-shot and LoRA fine-tuned
  • Significance tested with the Deflated Sharpe Ratio and block-bootstrap confidence intervals

LLMs · Finance · NLP

Fine-Tuning Small LLMs for Financial Sentiment

Parameter-efficient fine-tuning of Qwen2.5 (0.5B & 1.5B) for financial sentiment, then a look-ahead-free evaluation of the signal on real market data.

Approach

  • LoRA and QLoRA (4-bit NF4) adapters — only 0.28% of parameters trained
  • Trained on Twitter Financial News Sentiment; tested out-of-domain on Financial PhraseBank
  • QLoRA cuts memory by 55% while staying within 0.7 F1 points of LoRA
  • Look-ahead-free backtest on StockNet: 104k tweets, 87 stocks — sentiment tracks same-day returns (IC +0.073, t = 9.1)

Health · Time-series · IEEE AICCONF 2026

Predicting Feelings in Eating Disorders from Wearable Signals

First-author research: predicting anxiety, tiredness and fear of weight gain from Empatica E4 signals in individuals with atypical anorexia nervosa — toward real-time, personalized monitoring.

Approach

  • 7 signals at 4 Hz (HR, EDA, BVP, temperature, 3-axis motion), aligned with self-reports
  • Baseline normalization per participant to remove individual differences
  • Multisampling augmentation: 320 → 25,600 training sequences
  • XGBoost vs LSTM, then participant-specific fine-tuning
  • LSTM
